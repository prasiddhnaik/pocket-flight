F-15C gun effects

24 October 2026 · Local development · Ready for user visual testing

The solid cone flash has been replaced by a short irregular volume with a bright core, amber fringe, restrained local light and fading smoke. Every fifth simulated projectile now receives a saturated orange tracer head, red-orange edge and tapered optical glow. Tracer color bypasses scene tone mapping, and glow width is measured in CSS pixels for high-DPI displays. Projectile bodies remain 20 mm wide.

Research

47 downloaded images were visually screened across six labeled contact sheets. Six clear firing or smoke references were retained: one resolved M61 muzzle-flash photograph, one distant Hornet firing photograph and four F-5 smoke analogies. Two F-5 exposures are correlated. The remaining images were excluded from flash inference; many show afterburners, condensation or ordinary flybys. One failed download is not counted.

The main flash reference is the compact amber discharge in Makoto Morimoto’s Axalp firing gallery. Smoke comparisons come from Kedar Karmarkar’s Axalp photographs and Peter Steehouwer’s Axalp gallery. These are visual analogies, not measured F-15C flash calibration. F-5 images are not used to infer M61 flash dimensions.

Orange tracer correction

The previous HDR additive core could wash toward white against a bright sky. The glow now uses a saturated orange palette with normal alpha blending and no scene tone mapping. Twelve targeted checks and both builds passed after this correction. Visual flight testing remains with the user.
