Accuracy and test limits

All 440 saved solids are valid, closed, positive and connected by the declared physical contact tree. This verifies the authored digital model, not manufacturing conformity. Local section shapes remain photographic estimates, ruled shoulder joins are not certified C1 surfaces, and sampled motion checks do not establish a continuous collision proof. Full canopy/body opening checks sample 10°, 25°, 40° and 55° outside the designed 0.25 m hinge/rear-seal zone; the separate revised-frame sweep has no exclusion.

The wing reference-area/MAC discrepancy remains open: authored aerodynamic area about 61.56 m² and MAC 5.25 m versus the cited NASA 56.61 m² and 4.86 m. The wing-only low-speed vortex-lattice experiment is an attached-flow approximation, not full-aircraft CFD, inlet performance or flightworthiness evidence.

The gray faces visible deep inside the intake mouths are liner rear closures: the sampled face is 5.156 m aft of the local lip. Finite bent probes establish the tested reserved passage only; they terminate ahead of the rear closure.
