Peregrine: motion and effects

Shared by the aircraft inspection viewer and the flyable Peregrine. The home-screen aircraft selector switches between Finch and Peregrine. Flight supplies throttle, control inputs, damage and automatic gear commands; recorded effect state follows replay playback and video export.

SystemVisual design and behaviour
Engine startup / shutdownThrottle drives a gradual turbine spool. Intake rotors accelerate, nozzle glow grows, and exhaust heat develops. Shutdown winds the system down.
AfterburnerHigh-power gate, subtle blue fringe, amber-white core, volumetric tapered plume with integrated pressure cells and downstream flow variation, and warm illumination on the engine surrounds. Each plume is attached to the aircraft at its own exhaust outlet.
Heat distortionA restrained, depth-aware distortion behind the exhaust, using the rendered scene. Can be disabled independently. Disabled under reduced-motion preference.
Gear and canopySix wheels rotate about their axle centres. Main legs fold inward, nose gear folds aft, and bay doors close after retraction. Canopy opens upward on a fitted rear hinge and closes with running engines. A separate windscreen stays fixed, and the glazing perimeter follows the cockpit cut with a continuous weather seal.
Control surfacesDamped aileron, rudder and elevator movement, with separate slower flap travel. Preview sequences supply the corresponding control inputs.
Aircraft lightingRed port / green starboard lights, white tail and landing lights, pulsing red beacon, and paired white strobes. Reduced motion replaces flashing with steady low intensity.
Flight trailsEngine condensation is reserved for the high-altitude cruise preview, rather than triggered by speed alone; short wingtip vortices appear under stronger banking. Trails fade downstream and remain subtle.
TouchdownWheel spin, a brief tire-smoke puff, and a damped gear-compression response at the eight-second touchdown cue.
Engine troubleLeft-engine glow and afterburner fade, accompanied by a dark smoke trail. The right engine remains available.
AudioOptional synthesized turbine whine and low exhaust roar follow RPM and burner strength. Sound begins only through the sound button; pause and hidden-tab states mute it.

Preview sequences

Startup, takeoff, cruise, afterburner, touchdown, and engine trouble. Selecting a sequence selects a useful camera angle. Manual throttle and system controls override the sequence. Restart replays its cues. Pause freezes simulation time, and slow motion runs at quarter speed.

Runtime contract

JetInput carries throttle, burner, gear, canopy, lights, roll, pitch, yaw, flaps, speed, damage, and an explicit contrail-environment flag. JetEffects.update(dt, input) advances the effect system. A zero timestep freezes animation; touchdown() triggers the landing puff and compression. This separates the visual system from aircraft flight physics.

The GLB contains the corrected airframe and articulated object hierarchy. Flame shaders, procedural particles, heat distortion and sound live in the browser source, rather than being baked into the GLB.
