Pocket Flight: working weapons and combat range

31 October 2026 · Local, uncommitted implementation · Branch codex/private-wind-tunnel

Result

A separate Combat range launch sits beside existing flight choices. F-15C, Peregrine and Stormbird have functioning guns, unguided rockets and guided air-to-air training missiles. Finch A–2 consumes its concurrently authored hardpoint data and carries fourteen rockets in two outer pods, with no gun, missiles or bombs. Four visible range targets have damage, destruction, respawn and HUD feedback. The original F-15C cannon ballistics, discharge, physical rounds and optical tracers are reused.

How to play: choose a supported aircraft, select Combat range, hold J for the gun, press K for a rocket, or wait for LOCKED and press L for a missile. Pointer buttons are also present. R restarts/rearms; Esc pauses. The range is over open water and does not rewrite scenery or existing missions.

Limits: training ballistics, provisional fighter gun ports outside the F-15C, static targets, manual rocket aiming, no enemy AI/incoming fire, no bomb release, no loadout editor and no combat-event replay. Store mass/drag are designed but disabled; the metadata helper is not integrated into flight physics. Flight tuning and aircraft mass budgets were not changed.

Runtime evidence and its limits

During implementation the browser exposed a missing combat-menu icon, which was fixed. A development reload interrupted an earlier check, and one later firing wait exceeded 15 seconds; the final repeated run passed. These exploratory failures are distinct from the final recorded pass.

The browser workflow chooses the F-15C and enters combat through the actual UI. It then sets explicit aiming poses and fires with keyboard events through the running game loop. It checks gun damage, a rocket destroying a ground target, a locked missile destroying an air target, combat entry for the other fighters and Finch A–2, and immediate combat-state/HUD removal on return to free flight. This proves functional integration, not unassisted aiming usability, mobile touch acceptance or production behavior. The runner closes its own browser. The local server is stopped before handback.

My call

  1. Open-ended training sandbox, separate launch button, no score or victory screen.
  2. Air start over open water at the coordinates above; existing trim speed and flight handling.
  3. Fighters and attack Finch may enter; civilian/trainer variants cannot.
  4. Two static air and two ground targets, 500–900 m ahead; no AI or incoming fire.
  5. Three health points; gun damage one, ordnance damage three; five-second respawn; hits and destruction tracked separately.
  6. Spherical gameplay hit volumes, seven metres air/six ground; no area damage or blast radius.
  7. J holds gun, K releases rocket, L releases missile; separate on-screen buttons; one store release per press.
  8. Two four-round rocket pods per fighter and two seven-round pods on Finch; remaining fighter stations carry training missiles. Restart rearms everything.
  9. Fighter stations use measured visual mounts; all accept training rocket pods or air-to-air missiles. Finch accepts only its declared compatible loads.
  10. Keep the F-15C gun’s original ballistics/capacity. Other fighters temporarily share those values at provisional gun ports; Finch has no gun.
  11. Rocket 350 m/s plus aircraft velocity, gravity only, eight-second life; no wind/drag/burn model.
  12. Missile 520 m/s plus aircraft velocity, bounded 35°/s pursuit, twelve-second life, no gravity or speed decay.
  13. Missile nearest-air-target auto-selection, 12° cone, 30–2,500 m range, 0.6 s uninterrupted lock; no ground lock, countermeasures or retargeting.
  14. Gun’s existing 500 m reticle plus a store boresight; no predicted rocket-impact cue.
  15. Orange ground boxes, cyan air wire spheres, explicit labels/health/distance and short hit/destruction feedback.
  16. Hide display-only missiles and replace them with loadout-driven combat stores while in the range. Empty rocket pods stay attached.
  17. Keep bomb stations empty until bomb release is implemented.
  18. Store mass/drag remain off; no player combat damage or weapons replay recording in this slice.
