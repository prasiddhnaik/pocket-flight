Briefing

Summary

Over two days the game got new effects and sound across the fleet, the Heron was rebuilt as a Grand Caravan-style plane, the first island and its buildings were improved, and the scanned trees were replaced with very cheap models. The missile launch animations that opened the session were not built. The work is committed in one commit, together with the earlier uncommitted work it depends on.

Notices

Warnings and risks

  • The commit also holds 97 files of earlier work that this session did not touch, such as src/flight/combat/ and src/world/range-island.ts. This session's 65 files alone give 65 type errors, so they cannot go without it.
  • Four files were left out and stay uncommitted on disk: two partial PDF downloads in docs/research/f15c/sources/, scripts/.tmp-ray.ts, and the unused public/assets/scenery/tree-impostor-top.png. This report sits in the ignored output/ folder, so it was added with git add -f.
  • Missile launch animations were not built, and the missile flame and smoke are untouched. The plan for them is Task 5 in docs/plans/2026-11-01-f15c-blender-skin-implementation.html.
  • The 2 test failures are ctx.save is not a function at src/world/world.ts:318, seen only in the full suite. That call was already in commit 4239945, both tests pass alone, and a clean copy of the work fails the same way, so another test's stub is the likely cause. That cause is not confirmed, and commit 4239945 itself was not tested.
  • Gameplay changes: Peregrine and Stormbird bullets start 1 to 1.6 m from where they did. The tower, terminal, fuel farm, church, store, water tower and boathouse are now solid. There are 9,000 solid trees in new places instead of 6,500. Shrubs have no collision.
  • Heron differences left in place: the wing is drawn about 1.25 m behind where the physics calculates lift, the propeller 0.34 m below the thrust line and about 0.62 m off the ground, and the nose wheel about 0.65 m too far back, as you decided. Crash shapes follow the new body, so a nose-low landing can strike sooner.
  • Low flight gains least from the new trees: triangles fall only from 171,000 to 147,987, and draws rise from 14 to 17.
  • Rewinding a long flight may lose older trails sooner, because trails emit more particles. The worst case is about 25 seconds of history, roughly half. Aurelia is also very quiet at low power, at -46 dB at 12% power.
  • Corrections to figures given earlier in chat. The old tree download is 5.29 MB, not 5.76 MB, which counted images already inside the models plus a file made this session. The 10 buildings total 338 KB, not about 450 KB. The Heron's 11.5 and 9.6 MB were binary megabytes, and this report uses 12.11 and 10.04 MB.
  • Open flaws: faint ripple on the Heron's dorsal fillet, a thick antenna, no gun port on Peregrine or Stormbird, smooth grass at ground level, a dark streak beside the runway's north end, and hangars and cottages not remodelled.
