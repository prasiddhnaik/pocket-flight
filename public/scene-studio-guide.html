Before you start

Record a flight, then open Studio

Open Replays from the game menu. Add a saved flight to your film and open Scene Studio to edit its clips, objects, and camera.

Bring Blender assets as GLB

Export a GLB with textures and animation included. Scene Studio also supports glTF, OBJ, STL, images, and audio. Native .blend files are not imported.

Back up your browser projects

Your recordings and assets save in this browser on this site. Clearing site data removes them. Use Backup to keep a portable project with its recordings and assets, then Restore it on another browser.

Export with the tab visible

Select 720p or 1080p and a 30 or 60 fps target. Browser performance determines capture cadence. Keep the editor visible until rendering finishes.
